Residential three-day notice display standard, October 28

Confirmed against the full October 28 Anne-Michelle working session and her emailed corrected PDF. This is a display change; do not rewrite intake, change recipients or infer signing authority.

- Generate the approved exact PDF through `draft_case_notice`; Word is the editing source. Review/approve the PDF returned by `prepare_case_notice_review` after each saved edit.
- Every inserted value uses embedded Times New Roman at 12pt in the PDF and explicit Times New Roman 12pt in Word. Never shrink text to fit.
- Keep the premises street/unit on one line and City, ST ZIP on the next. Payment and housing-provider blocks follow the same pattern. `PO BOX 12345 SAN JOSE, CA 95152` must print `P.O. Box 12345` / `San Jose, CA 95152`.
- Tenant names use normal capitalization and comma separators. Print `All Other Occupants` with those capitals.
- The heading is `TIME PERIOD DUE`, underlined across all three words. Monthly dated entries print the whole calendar month (e.g. `November 1-30, 2025`), including leap years. Keep explicit partial periods; never guess missing years.
- An informal family relationship title prints `Authorized Agent for Property Owner` (final wording at 34:07). An actual `Property Owner` remains `Property Owner`; formal titles such as `Property Manager` or `Trustee` remain formal titles. Keep signer identity separate from owner/payee.
- Retain separate time-period and amount columns, every submitted rent entry and a reconciled total. Long lists use the existing measured paginator, keeping whole paragraphs and the signature/provider block together.
- Every new/edited notice goes into the case's verified address folder in Orders OneDrive: Documents > Blueprint Evictions > address. Return the PDF link, Word editing link and folder link in chat. Never claim success from a folder's existence alone.
- A generator version change invalidates a previously rendered PDF for approval. Obtain a fresh PDF review; never silently send an old approval through a new renderer.

Versions: Word `residential-pay-or-quit-2026-10-28-format3`; PDF `residential-original-pdf-2026-10-28-format3`. Other notice families, historical documents, business credentials and delivery/paid-signing controls are outside this change.
